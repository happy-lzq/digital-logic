Boolean Algebra
◆ Principle of Duality (对偶性原理)
Given a logic expression, its dual (对偶) is obtained by: 
➢ replacing all “+” with “·”
➢ replacing all “·” with “+”
➢ replacing all “0” with “1”
➢ replacing all “1” with “0”
A true statement’s dual is also a true statement !
